GRENSARBEID.ORG — System-Audit

Cross-border jurisdictional vacuum in social security — an open-source X-ray of two national systems

Author: Eric Messa • Version updated: 6 november 2026, 13:03

Language & Translation Notice: Dutch (nl/) is the sole authentic source language of this System-Audit. The automated translations of the overview, documents, visual architecture, synthesis and rights matrix are provided to make the documented cross-border mechanisms accessible across Europe. In case of any ambiguity or legal interpretation, the original Dutch text and source files prevail.

Not an advice desk and not a band-aid on a stalled form. Through one fully documented case (62 institutions, over 700 actions), this site shows how the Belgian and Dutch systems actually interact behind the scenes — where they lock up, why official letters contradict each other, and which levers actually work.

Each of the six documents is available in three formats: as a web page (readable directly in the browser), as a Word document (.docx, for editing or printing), and as a PDF (for fixed layout or archiving).
